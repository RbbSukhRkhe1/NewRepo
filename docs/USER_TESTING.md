## Task list (read to participant)

1. **Orientation:** “Imagine you want to donate to a cause you care about. This is a student capstone project.”
2. On the **home page**, read the headline and **one** beneficiary story in the carousel (use arrows or wait for auto-advance).
3. Scroll to **How it works** and say which step you would do first.
4. Open **Causes** and pick a campaign to read.
5. **Register** a new account *or* log in with the demo account the facilitator provides.
6. Open the **Ledger** and explain in one sentence what you think it shows.
7. (Optional) Open **Impact** and say how it differs from the home page stories.
8. (Optional) Open **Account** after logging in.

**Success criteria:** completes tasks 2–6 without unrecoverable confusion.
