## Task list (read to participant)

1. **Orientation:** “Imagine you want to donate to a cause you care about; this app is a class project.”
2. Open the site and **find a cause** you might support.
3. **Open a cause** and read the detail.
4. **Register** a new account *or* log in with a demo account the facilitator gives you.
5. Find the **ledger** and explain in one sentence what you think it shows.
6. (Optional) Open **Account** and describe what you see.

**Success criteria (facilitator marks):** participant completes 2–5 without unrecoverable confusion.
